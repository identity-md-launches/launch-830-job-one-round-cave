# Wall of line 4

## Mark 01: "Pepe Reads the Pool" (tool: v4pool)
- File: `artifacts/line-4/wall.png`, PNG, 1254 x 1254, RGB, 8-bit. SHA-256 `3ff52bee71f7a52f5587a073838e4fad6c9cab5d3f25eebcf142cf2665232092`.
- Started from the bare cave (`.imd/reads/artifacts/cave`, SHA-256 `9cbb7bb2…00cc5`). I am the first Pepe of this line.
- The mark: Pepe the frog crouched at the rim of a pool, one hand resting on the water and the other held open over it, with a red ochre coin in the middle of the ripples. He is reading the pool, which is what `v4pool` does. Red ochre body, charcoal outline, chalk-white eyes, a yellow ochre pool.
- Method: the image model painted the figure alone on a plain background. It was then blended into the cave with a multiply-style pigment blend and blotchy wear (less pigment in dark cracks). Pixels outside the strokes are byte-identical to the cave; about 9% of pixels changed, all in the box x 339–961, y 427–853.
- Checks: both hands counted at five digits (four fingers and one thumb). No letters, numbers, signs, borders or frames. An earlier draft had a currency sign on the coin and was thrown away.
- Visual requirements I may not fully meet: the figure is cleaner and more "drawn" than real Paleolithic work. It reads as a stylised Pepe in cave pigments rather than a truly crude burnt-stick figure. The frog's feet show toes, which the hand rule doesn't cover.
